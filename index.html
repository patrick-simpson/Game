ULTRA MAZE DRIVER

JUNGLE RESCUE — FIRST-PERSON DRIVE

A VIP is lost deep in the jungle. You're behind the wheel — drive the corridor and take the right turns to reach them.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Goal: follow the path through the jungle and reach the VIP.
  • Steer with ←/→ or the wheel; GO / FAST / SLOW / STOP to drive, BOOST for speed.
  • LASER blasts rogue cars & monsters; JUMP hops obstacles; REVERSE backs up.
  • Take the right turns — wrong ones loop back. Don't drive into the VOID or you start over!
  • Grab glowing green gems for bonus points. Use the mini-map (voids show as red pits).
DIFFICULTY

Easy: short & straight · Normal: longer route · Hard: many decoys & voids
